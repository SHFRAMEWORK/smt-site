Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Social Media Tool ("the app") is a private desktop application used by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app requests these Google permissions:

  • youtube.readonly: to read the connected channel's ID, name, picture and basic statistics, so the owner can confirm the right channel, and to read back uploaded videos to confirm they were processed with the intended privacy setting.
  • youtube.upload: to upload the owner's videos, with the title, description and tags the owner chose.

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, files, comments, viewers' data or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to upload and verify the owner's videos on the owner's channels. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with or transferred to any third party, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored

Sign-in tokens and channel details are stored only on the owner's own Windows computer. Tokens are encrypted with Windows data protection for the signed-in Windows user. The app has no server and does not send this data anywhere except directly to Google's own YouTube API.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while a channel is connected. Choosing Disconnect in the app revokes the access at Google and deletes the stored token and channel details from the computer. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploading to YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

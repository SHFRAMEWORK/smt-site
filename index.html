Social Media Tool

A private desktop app for publishing the owner's own short science videos.

What it does

Social Media Tool runs on its owner's Windows PC. It watches a folder of finished videos and uploads each one, on a schedule, to the owner's own YouTube channels (Galaxling and Whylaxy), with the title, description and tags the owner set.

It is a personal tool: it is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and it only ever acts on channels the owner has connected and approved.

How it uses Google

  • The owner signs in with Google and picks a channel. The app asks for two YouTube permissions: to see the channel (so it can confirm it is the right one and check uploads) and to upload videos.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and are never sent anywhere else.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
